# Frequently asked questions

These are the questions people search for most about the Wonderlic, taken from Google's "People also ask" boxes and related searches for US searches on "wonderlic test" and "wonderlic score" in November 2026, plus the questions Wonderlic answers in its own candidate FAQ. Answers stick to what Wonderlic states, and say so when the answer is our guidance.

## What is the Wonderlic test?

A pre-employment assessment from Wonderlic, Inc. The current version, Wonderlic Select, has three parts: a timed cognitive ability test of 50 questions in 12 minutes, and untimed motivation and personality questionnaires. The full assessment takes about 35 minutes on average. See the [test overview](test-overview.md).

## How many questions are on the Wonderlic, and how long does it take?

The cognitive section has 50 questions and a 12-minute limit. The motivation section has 58 items and the personality section has 150, both untimed. Wonderlic says most people finish the whole assessment in about 35 minutes.

## What is a good Wonderlic score?

Wonderlic says there is no universal "good" or "average" score for Wonderlic Select. A good result is one that fits the job you applied for, and employers compare your results with that job's profile. For practice, our guidance is to aim for the mid to high 20s or better on a 50-question, 12-minute test scored by number correct. See [how scoring works](scoring.md#what-counts-as-a-good-score).

## What is the average score on the Wonderlic test?

On the classic 50-question cognitive test, Wonderlic says the average score is around 20. Wonderlic Select does not give candidates a single numerical cognitive score, so there is no comparable average for it.

## How can I pass the Wonderlic test?

There is nothing to pass: Wonderlic says the assessment has no passing or failing grade. To do your best on the timed part:

1. Learn the six question types and their traps, using the [section guides](test-overview.md#the-cognitive-section).
2. Practise mental math, since calculators are not allowed.
3. Do at least two full timed runs of 50 questions in 12 minutes before the real one.
4. On the day, make your best guess on slow questions and move on, as Wonderlic advises.

The [study plans](study-plans.md) set this out day by day.

## Is there a pass mark?

No. Each employer decides how to use the results for each role, and Wonderlic Select combines your cognitive, motivation and personality results rather than applying one cut-off. See [how scoring works](scoring.md#is-there-a-pass-mark).

## Is the Wonderlic hard?

Wonderlic's own answer is that it is "not hard but it is challenging." The timed part is designed so that everyone finds some questions difficult, the questions get harder as you go, and test takers are not expected to answer all 50 in time. The individual questions use everyday math and vocabulary. The difficulty is the pace.

## Can you use a calculator on the Wonderlic?

No. Wonderlic says a calculator would not significantly improve your results and might slow you down, and that only a small number of questions involve calculations. Practise mental math with the [math guide](sections/math-word-problems.md).

## Is the Wonderlic an IQ test?

Wonderlic describes it as a cognitive ability test that measures your ability "to learn, adapt, and solve problems." It does not call it an IQ test on its candidate pages. The comparison is common online because both measure general reasoning, but the Wonderlic is built and scored for hiring, against job profiles.

## What did Tom Brady, Patrick Mahomes or other NFL players score on the Wonderlic?

These questions, including ones about Rob Gronkowski, Travis Kelce and Tim Tebow, come up constantly in searches. We do not list individual scores because none of them are official. The NFL treated Wonderlic scores as confidential: a 2012 NFL.com report quotes a memo from Commissioner Roger Goodell saying they are "strictly confidential for club use only" ([NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/wonderlic-scores-other-draft-info-is-confidential-goodell-says-09000d5d8281ad7a)). The numbers online are leaks or media reports that cannot be checked against a primary source. They also come from older versions of the test, so they tell you little about what a given employer wants today.

## Does the NFL still use the Wonderlic?

News reports in 2022 said the NFL had stopped using it at the Scouting Combine. We could not confirm this on an NFL or Wonderlic page, so treat it as reported rather than official.

## Can I see my Wonderlic score?

Usually not as a number. Wonderlic says the only result candidates get is a Candidate Feedback Report, through a link at the end of the assessment, if the employer provides one. Numerical cognitive scores are not available to candidates. Employers see results immediately.

## Is the Wonderlic proctored?

Typically not. Wonderlic says companies usually do not proctor Wonderlic Select, though some choose to.

## Can I retake the Wonderlic?

Wonderlic says retake policies are set by the employer, not by Wonderlic. Ask your recruiter or hiring contact.

## My test link has expired or says "Assessment not Available". What now?

Links generally stay active for a maximum of 14 days. If you see "Assessment not Available" earlier, the employer has disabled the assessment. Wonderlic cannot resend invitations, so contact the hiring organisation.

## Does the Wonderlic cost anything?

Not for test takers. Wonderlic says it does not sell assessments to individuals, only to organisations, and warns candidates to be cautious if anyone asks them to pay for a Wonderlic test.

## Are there real Wonderlic questions or answer PDFs online?

Wonderlic publishes sample questions and a short practice test, but the questions on the real assessment are not published. Documents claiming to be real tests cannot be verified, and the official practice test itself draws from a pool so its questions change. Learning the question types is a better use of time. Every practice item in these notes is original.

## Is there an official practice test?

Yes. Wonderlic's [test-taker hub](https://wonderlic.com/test-takers/) has a free timed practice test with instant feedback: 5 questions in 72 seconds, drawn from a larger pool, with no sign-up.

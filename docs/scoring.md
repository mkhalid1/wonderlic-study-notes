# How Wonderlic scoring works

Short version: on today's Wonderlic Select there is no pass mark and no single number you are told. Your cognitive answers are scored with each question weighted by difficulty, combined with your motivation and personality results, and compared with the profile of the job you applied for. The famous "out of 50, average around 20" figure belongs to the classic version of the test.

## Two kinds of score, two versions of the test

| | Classic 50-question cognitive test | Wonderlic Select |
|---|---|---|
| What is scored | Number of correct answers | Your overall response pattern, weighting each question by its difficulty and the effect of guessing |
| Scale | Out of 50 | No single numerical cognitive score is reported to you |
| Average | Around 20, according to Wonderlic | Wonderlic says there is no universal "good" or "average" score |
| Combined with other parts? | No | Yes, with motivation and personality, against the demands of the job |

Wonderlic explains the change on its cognitive ability page: weighting each question by difficulty gives "a more precise estimate" than a score based only on "the total number of correct answers." It also says the questions get harder as the test goes on.

## How the job profile works

Wonderlic says its scoring compares candidates with what a specific role demands. On its cognitive ability page it says:

- the weight given to the cognitive score, compared with personality and motivation, varies by job
- job profiles are built from the U.S. Department of Labor's O\*NET data and Wonderlic's own applicant data

So the same performance can be a strong fit for one role and an ordinary one for another. Wonderlic's test-taker page puts it plainly: "good" is relative to the role.

## What you and the employer see

- **The employer** has immediate access to your results when you finish. Wonderlic's employer page describes ranked candidate lists, side-by-side comparisons and structured interview guides.
- **You** may get a **Candidate Feedback Report**, through a link at the end of the assessment, if the employer offers one. Wonderlic says this is the only result given to candidates, and that numerical cognitive scores are not available to them. If no link appeared when you finished, no report is available.
- Wonderlic notes that prevailing laws generally do not require employers to share results with candidates.

## Is there a pass mark?

No. Wonderlic's candidate FAQ says there is no "passing" or "failing" grade with Wonderlic Select. Each employer decides how to use the results for each role.

Numbers you will see online, such as score bands or "the score you need for job X", come from prep sites, old tables or NFL reports. Wonderlic does not publish them on its current candidate pages, so treat them as guesswork.

## Should you guess?

Wonderlic's own tip is: if a question takes too long, "make your best guess and move on." Its scoring takes "the effect of guessing" into account, but it does not publish exactly how a blank compares with a wrong answer. Follow the publisher's advice: a reasoned guess after eliminating one or two options, then the next question.

Wonderlic also advises against "skipping around" hoping to come back to questions. Plan to answer in order.

## What counts as a good score

Since you will not see a number on the real test, the useful question is what to aim for in practice. As study guidance, not a published standard:

- **On a 50-question, 12-minute practice test scored by number correct,** Wonderlic's classic average of around 20 is your reference point. Scoring in the mid to high 20s or above in timed practice puts you clearly above it, with a margin for nerves.
- **Watch accuracy as well as reach.** Reaching more questions only helps if most of the extra answers are right. The early questions are the easier ones, so careless misses there are the cheapest marks to lose. In practice, note both how many you answered and how many you got right.
- **Keep your practice honest:** no calculator, no pausing, one sitting. The [free PrepClubs Wonderlic practice test](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=wonderlic) is timed like the real cognitive section.

## Why practice scores vary

Practice tests from different sources differ in difficulty, and the official Wonderlic practice test draws from a larger pool, so its questions change each time. A swing of a few marks between attempts is normal. Track the trend over several runs, and track which question types you miss, not just the total.

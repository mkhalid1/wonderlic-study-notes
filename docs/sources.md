# Sources

Every fact in these notes about the test itself comes from Wonderlic's own pages, with one football history point from NFL.com. Each source below was checked live on 24 November 2026. If something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/wonderlic-study-notes/issues).

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not publisher statements, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the split of the cognitive section into six question types, and how they are taught
- the per-type timing targets and clock checkpoints
- the practice target of the mid to high 20s on a 50-question timed practice test
- the methods, traps lists, study plans and advice on the untimed sections
- all practice items and explanations, which are original

## What is not published

Wonderlic's current candidate pages do not publish:

- how many questions of each type appear in the cognitive section
- the exact scoring formula, or how a blank compares with a wrong answer
- which languages the assessment is offered in
- whether scratch paper is allowed
- details of shorter or older versions of the test

We say so wherever these come up, rather than quoting figures from prep sites.

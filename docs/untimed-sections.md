# The untimed sections

Wonderlic Select has two more parts besides the timed cognitive test: a motivation questionnaire of 58 items and a personality questionnaire of 150 items. Neither is timed, and Wonderlic says neither has right or wrong answers. Together they make up most of the roughly 35 minutes the full assessment takes.

These notes cannot give you practice items for these parts, because there is nothing to get right. What helps is knowing what they are for and how to approach them.

## What they measure

| Part | Items | What Wonderlic says it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Motivation (vocational interests) | 58 | The types of work and activities you find most interesting and engaging |
| Personality | 150 | How you react to people, tasks and situations in the workplace |

Wonderlic combines these results with your cognitive score and compares the combination with the demands of the job. How much each part counts depends on the role.

## How to approach them

- **Answer honestly and quickly.** These are statements about what you like and how you tend to behave. Your first honest reaction is the answer they are designed to collect.
- **Do not try to guess the "ideal" profile.** You do not know how the employer's job profile is built, and answers that try to look perfect tend to be inconsistent with each other.
- **Think about yourself at work,** not at home or at your best day. The personality part is about workplace situations.
- **Stay consistent.** Questionnaires of this length often ask about similar traits in different ways. Answer each one as it is written, not by trying to remember your earlier answers.
- **Take a breath between parts.** If the timed section went badly, it does not affect how you should answer these. Reset before you start them.

## Things to check before you start

- **Your link.** Links generally stay active for a maximum of 14 days. Leave enough time to finish all three parts in one sitting.
- **Your time.** Plan for about 35 minutes in total, which is Wonderlic's average for the whole assessment. Some people take longer on the untimed parts, and that is fine.
- **Accommodations.** If you need one for any part, ask the employer before you start. Wonderlic says employers arrange accommodations under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

## Where the timed part fits

Only the cognitive section has right and wrong answers, and it is the only part you can really train for. If your study time is limited, spend it on the [six question types](test-overview.md#the-cognitive-section) and on [timing](timing.md), and treat the untimed parts as a calm, honest questionnaire.

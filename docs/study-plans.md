# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both start with the [10-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weak question types. Expect 30 to 60 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

Remember the clock on the test link: Wonderlic says links generally stay active for a maximum of 14 days. Check your invitation for the date and plan backwards from it.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest question types and whether you ran out of time. |
| 2 | Math | Read the [math guide](sections/math-word-problems.md) and do its 5 items without a calculator. Spend 10 extra minutes on percentages and fractions in your head. |
| 3 | Series and logic | Read the [series](sections/number-series.md) and [logic](sections/logic.md) guides. Do both sets. Learn the series rule table and the logic patterns table. |
| 4 | Words | Read the [vocabulary](sections/vocabulary.md) and [analogies](sections/analogies.md) guides. Do both sets. Note every word you could not define. |
| 5 | First timed run | Read the [spatial guide](sections/spatial-reasoning.md) and do its set. Then take a full timed test of 50 questions in 12 minutes, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vmh9rf&utm_content=wonderlic). Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 6 | Fix the weakest type | Go back to your weakest type's traps list. Redo its items without the answers. Find at least 20 fresh items of that type and do them against the clock. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second full timed test in the morning. Compare your checkpoints and score with day 5. Try the official 5-question practice on the [Wonderlic test-taker hub](https://wonderlic.com/test-takers/). Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

The review is where the improvement comes from. For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Which rule did I break?** For example: took the percentage of the wrong number, fitted only the last two terms, answered "closest" on an "opposite" item.
2. **What in the question should have stopped me?** Name the word or number.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "write the gaps" or "read the instruction word first".

Keep these in one list. By day 6 you will see the same two or three mistakes repeating, and fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the link has arrived and you want to sit the test soon.

### Day 1: learn the six types

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 20 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of all six section guides. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 30 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest types. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 4 | 15 min | Take a full timed practice test of 50 questions in 12 minutes, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vmh9rf&utm_content=wonderlic). |
| 5 | 15 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Redo the items you missed yesterday, without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 5 min | Warm up with the official 5-question practice on the [Wonderlic test-taker hub](https://wonderlic.com/test-takers/). |
| 4 | About 35 min | Sit the real assessment in a quiet room, at a time when you are usually sharp. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then sit the test the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" questions or answer PDFs.** The cognitive questions on Wonderlic Select are not published, and lists that claim to be real are not something you can check. Learn the six types instead.
- **Do not practise with a calculator.** The real test does not allow one.
- **Do not cram the night before.** Speed on this test depends on a rested head.
- **Do not plan to come back to questions.** Practise answering in order, the way Wonderlic advises.

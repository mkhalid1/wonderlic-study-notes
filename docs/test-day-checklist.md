# Test-day checklist

The Wonderlic Select assessment is usually taken online, on your own device, at a time you choose within the link's window. That freedom means the conditions are up to you. Set them up the way you would for an exam.

## The day before

- [ ] Check the date your link expires. Wonderlic says links generally stay active for a maximum of 14 days.
- [ ] Block out about 35 minutes, Wonderlic's average for the full assessment, plus a 10-minute buffer.
- [ ] If you need an accommodation, ask the employer now. Wonderlic says employers arrange them, after consulting Wonderlic.
- [ ] Do a short practice run, then stop. A tired head loses more marks than a rusty one.

## One hour before

- [ ] Choose a quiet room where nobody will interrupt you for 45 minutes.
- [ ] Use a reliable internet connection. Wonderlic's own tips include a stable connection.
- [ ] Charge your device or plug it in. Close other apps and browser tabs.
- [ ] Put your phone away, on silent and out of reach.
- [ ] Have water nearby. Go to the bathroom.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the on-screen instructions fully. They tell you how the version you are taking works.
- [ ] Take a few slow breaths before you start the timed part. Wonderlic suggests this too.
- [ ] Remind yourself: you are not expected to finish all 50 questions.

## During the timed section

- [ ] Read each question once, carefully. Wonderlic says many mistakes come from misreading.
- [ ] Check the instruction word on vocabulary items: closest, opposite, similar, neither.
- [ ] If a question is taking too long, make your best guess and move on.
- [ ] Answer in order. Do not plan to come back.
- [ ] Glance at the clock at 3, 6 and 9 minutes. See the [pacing plan](timing.md#a-pacing-plan-for-12-minutes).
- [ ] No calculator.

## During the untimed sections

- [ ] Answer honestly, as you are at work. See the [untimed sections guide](untimed-sections.md).
- [ ] Do not rush, and do not overthink. There are no right or wrong answers here.

## Afterwards

- [ ] If a link to a Candidate Feedback Report appears, open it and save a copy. Wonderlic says it is the only result candidates receive.
- [ ] If something went wrong, such as a dropped connection, tell the employer straight away. Wonderlic cannot resend invitations, so the employer is the one who can help.
- [ ] Ask the employer, not Wonderlic, if you want to know whether a retake is possible.

## Last practice before the real one

A single timed run the day before, at the same time of day as your planned test, is the best rehearsal. Use the [free Wonderlic practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vmh9rf&utm_content=wonderlic) for 50 questions in 12 minutes, or the official 5-question practice on the [Wonderlic test-taker hub](https://wonderlic.com/test-takers/) as a warm-up.

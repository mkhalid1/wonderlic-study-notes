# Wonderlic Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for the Wonderlic cognitive ability test, the timed part of Wonderlic Select: how the test works, a clear method for each of the six question types, 42 original practice items with full explanations, how scoring really works on today's version, and study plans for a week or a weekend. Written for job applicants who have just received a Wonderlic link and want to use the days before it expires well. Facts about the test come only from Wonderlic's own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed Wonderlic practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=wonderlic), 50 questions in 12 minutes, or the shorter [10-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find your weak question types.** Take the [10-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has two items of each type and tells you where to start.
2. **Fix the weakest type first.** Open its guide below, learn the method and the traps, then do its 5 practice items.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your link has already arrived.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Wonderlic, Inc. |
| Current assessment | Wonderlic Select (formerly Wonscore) |
| Cognitive section | 50 questions in 12 minutes, timed |
| Other sections | Motivation (58 items) and personality (150 items), both untimed |
| Total time | About 35 minutes on average |
| Topics | Math and word problems, number series, vocabulary, analogies, logic, spatial reasoning |
| Calculator | Not allowed |
| Finishing | You are not expected to answer all 50 |
| Pass mark | None. Results are compared with the job's profile |
| Your result | A Candidate Feedback Report if the employer offers one. No numerical cognitive score |
| Classic average | Around 20 out of 50 on the classic cognitive test |

Sources: [Wonderlic Select candidate FAQ](https://wonderlic.com/blog/candidates/wonderlic-select-test-frequently-asked-questions-from-candidates/), [Wonderlic test-taker hub](https://wonderlic.com/test-takers/) and [Wonderlic cognitive ability page](https://wonderlic.com/science/cognitive-ability/), checked 24 November 2026.

## The six question types at a glance

| Question type | You do | Typical time | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Math and word problems | Mental arithmetic on prices, rates, ratios, averages | 10 to 20 s | [Math](docs/sections/math-word-problems.md) |
| Number and letter series | Find the rule and the next term | 10 to 15 s | [Series](docs/sections/number-series.md) |
| Vocabulary and word meaning | Closest or opposite meaning, sentence meaning | 5 to 10 s | [Vocabulary](docs/sections/vocabulary.md) |
| Analogies | Match the relationship between two words | 8 to 12 s | [Analogies](docs/sections/analogies.md) |
| Logic puzzles | True, false or not certain; orderings and seatings | 10 to 40 s | [Logic](docs/sections/logic.md) |
| Spatial reasoning | Directions, rotations, folding, counting shapes | 10 to 20 s | [Spatial](docs/sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |

Typical times are our study targets. Every guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 5 original practice items with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **No calculator.** The real test does not allow one, so practise without it from the start.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most explanations say why the nearest wrong answer is wrong, which is where the learning is.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write the rule you broke. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the methods. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/wonderlic?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=wonderlic), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want to annotate your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All 42 practice items (30 in the section guides and 12 in the diagnostic) are original, written for these notes. None are taken from the Wonderlic, from Wonderlic's materials or from any prep site. They follow the topics Wonderlic publishes so that the methods you learn here transfer to the real test.
